What you need to know

From registration to the closing ceremony

#01

No experience required

Prior debate or Model United Nations experience is not required. First-time and experienced delegates are both welcome.

#02
#03

Dress for committee

Formal business attire is required. Sneakers, shorts, and casual or brightly coloured clothing are not permitted.

Before committee

Prepare your brief

Allocations and papers come after you register

#04

Countries are assigned later

Country allocations and committee details are released once registration closes. The Secretariat notifies every participant.

#05
#06

Position papers are required

Every delegate submits a position paper by the deadline. Papers are part of preparation and evaluation.

Conference days

Two days in session

2 and 3 October 2026

#07

Mark the weekend

BMUN 2026 is held on 2nd and 3rd October 2026. Venue details and the full schedule are shared as preparations progress.

#08
#09

Awards on the floor

Recognition includes Best Delegate, Best Researcher, Honourable Mention, Best Speaker, and Most Diplomatic.
