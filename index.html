Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the core procedural and operational parameters of our assembly.

Why participate in BMUN?

BMUN offers a unique opportunity to develop essential skills such as public speaking, negotiation, and leadership, all while deepening your understanding of pressing global issues.

Eligibility & Standards

School and Grades 6–12 from all other institutions. We seek participants who demonstrate a strong commitment to diplomacy, critical thinking, and global awareness.

What is the dress code?

The dress code is formal and professional. Suits with a collared shirt and tie for males, and formal dresses, suits, or blazers with trousers for females.
